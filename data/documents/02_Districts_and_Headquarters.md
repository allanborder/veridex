# Tamil Nadu — Districts and Headquarters

## Section 1: Number of Districts

Tamil Nadu is administratively divided into 38 districts as of 2022. This follows a series of bifurcations and the creation of new districts to improve administrative efficiency and governance. The state previously had 32 districts before the addition of new ones in recent years. Each district is administered by a District Collector, an Indian Administrative Service (IAS) officer who is responsible for overall administration, law and order, and the implementation of government schemes.

## Section 2: Northern Districts

The northern region of Tamil Nadu includes districts such as Chennai, Tiruvallur, Kancheepuram, Chengalpattu, and Vellore. Chennai is the capital district, while Tiruvallur serves as the headquarters for the Tiruvallur district. Kancheepuram district, known for its silk sarees and ancient temples, has Kancheepuram as its headquarters. Chengalpattu district, newly carved out in 2019, has Chengalpattu as its headquarters. Vellore district has Vellore city as its headquarters.

## Section 3: Central Districts

The central region includes Tiruchirappalli (Trichy), Karur, Perambalur, Ariyalur, and Thanjavur. Tiruchirappalli district's headquarters is Tiruchirappalli city, a major industrial and educational hub. Thanjavur, historically known as the rice bowl of Tamil Nadu, has Thanjavur as its headquarters. Ariyalur district has Ariyalur town as its headquarters.

## Section 4: Western and Nilgiris Districts

Western Tamil Nadu includes Coimbatore, Tiruppur, Erode, Salem, Dharmapuri, Krishnagiri, and the Nilgiris. Coimbatore district has Coimbatore city as its headquarters and is known as the Manchester of South India for its textile industries. Tiruppur district, carved out from Coimbatore, has Tiruppur city as its headquarters and is the knitwear export capital of India. Erode district has Erode city as its headquarters. The Nilgiris district, home to hill stations like Ooty (Udhagamandalam), has Udhagamandalam as its headquarters.

## Section 5: Southern Districts

The southernmost districts include Madurai, Virudhunagar, Theni, Dindigul, Ramanathapuram, Thoothukudi, Tirunelveli, Tenkasi, and Kanyakumari. Madurai district has Madurai city as its headquarters, famous for the Meenakshi Amman Temple. Kanyakumari district is the southernmost district of mainland India, with Nagercoil as its headquarters. The headquarters of Thoothukudi district is Thoothukudi (Tuticorin) city, a major port city.

## Section 6: Eastern Districts and Delta Region

Eastern Tamil Nadu includes Cuddalore, Villupuram, Kallakurichi, Nagapattinam, Mayiladuthurai, Tiruvarur, and Pudukkottai. Nagapattinam district, located on the Coromandel Coast, has Nagapattinam as its headquarters. Mayiladuthurai district, newly created in 2020, has Mayiladuthurai as its headquarters. The delta districts of Nagapattinam, Tiruvarur, and Thanjavur form the fertile Cauvery Delta region, the most agriculturally productive area of Tamil Nadu.
