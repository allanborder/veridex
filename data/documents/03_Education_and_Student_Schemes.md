# Tamil Nadu — Education and Student Schemes

## Section 1: Overview of Education System

Tamil Nadu has one of the strongest public education systems in India. The state government operates a wide network of government schools, government-aided schools, and higher education institutions. Tamil Nadu has a gross enrolment ratio in higher education that is among the highest in the country. The state is home to prominent universities including Anna University (Chennai), Madurai Kamaraj University, Bharathiar University (Coimbatore), and Bharathidasan University (Tiruchirappalli).

## Section 2: Pudhumai Penn Scheme

Pudhumai Penn (meaning "New/Modern Woman" in Tamil) is a flagship scheme of the Tamil Nadu state government designed to encourage girls to pursue higher education. Under the Pudhumai Penn scheme, girl students who have studied from Class 6 to Class 12 in government schools are eligible to receive a monthly stipend of Rs. 1,000 when they join government colleges or government-aided colleges for higher education. The scheme aims to reduce dropout rates among girl students and increase female participation in higher education across Tamil Nadu.

## Section 3: Chief Minister's Breakfast Scheme

The Chief Minister's Breakfast Scheme was launched in Tamil Nadu to provide free nutritious breakfast to students studying in government primary schools. The scheme was introduced to address hunger and malnutrition among school-going children, particularly in rural and economically weaker sections. Under this scheme, students in Classes 1 to 5 in government primary schools receive a hot and nutritious breakfast before classes begin each morning. The scheme supplements the existing mid-day meal programme.

## Section 4: Free Uniform and Textbook Scheme

The Tamil Nadu government provides free uniforms, footwear, school bags, notebooks, and textbooks to students enrolled in government schools. Students receive two sets of uniforms per academic year. This scheme reduces the financial burden on families from low-income backgrounds and helps improve school attendance rates.

## Section 5: Higher Education and Engineering Colleges

Tamil Nadu has the highest number of engineering colleges in India. Anna University, the affiliating university for engineering and technology colleges in the state, affiliates over 500 engineering colleges across Tamil Nadu. The Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions (TNEA) process manages centralised counselling for admission to undergraduate engineering programmes. The state also operates Government Arts and Science Colleges in each district to ensure access to affordable higher education.

## Section 6: Scholarship Programmes

Several scholarship programmes are available to students in Tamil Nadu. The Tamil Nadu Government provides post-matric scholarships to students from Scheduled Caste (SC), Scheduled Tribe (ST), Most Backward Classes (MBC), and Other Backward Classes (OBC) communities pursuing higher education. The Ambedkar Fellowship, named after Dr. B. R. Ambedkar, supports meritorious students from economically weaker sections in pursuing professional and research-oriented courses.
