# Tamil Nadu — Health and Public Health

## Section 1: Healthcare Infrastructure

Tamil Nadu is widely regarded as having one of the best public healthcare systems among all Indian states. The state operates a three-tier healthcare infrastructure consisting of Sub-Centres at the village level, Primary Health Centres (PHCs) at the block level, and Government District Hospitals and Medical College Hospitals at the district level. Tamil Nadu has more than 1,700 Primary Health Centres spread across its districts. The state's capital, Chennai, is a major medical tourism destination and is home to globally reputed multi-speciality hospitals.

## Section 2: Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme

The Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme (CMCHIS), popularly known as the Kalaignar Insurance Scheme, provides cashless healthcare coverage to families below a specified income threshold. The scheme covers a wide range of medical procedures including cardiac surgeries, cancer treatment, orthopaedic procedures, neurological treatment, and kidney transplants. The scheme is implemented through empanelled government and private hospitals across Tamil Nadu, ensuring that even rural residents can access tertiary care without incurring catastrophic health expenditure.

## Section 3: Makkalai Thedi Maruthuvam

Makkalai Thedi Maruthuvam (MTM) — meaning "Healthcare Reaching the People" — is a community health outreach programme launched by the Tamil Nadu government. Under this programme, trained healthcare workers visit households to provide basic health screening, chronic disease management, and referral services. The programme targets patients with non-communicable diseases such as diabetes, hypertension, and cardiovascular conditions who may not actively seek medical care. MTM has been widely recognised as a model for proactive community healthcare delivery in India.

## Section 4: Tamil Nadu Medical Services Corporation

The Tamil Nadu Medical Services Corporation (TNMSC) is a state-owned entity responsible for the procurement and supply of medicines, surgical items, and medical equipment to all government healthcare institutions in Tamil Nadu. TNMSC follows a competitive tendering process to procure quality medicines at affordable prices. Tamil Nadu's free medicine scheme through TNMSC has been nationally recognised as a model for ensuring the availability of essential medicines in government hospitals at no cost to patients.

## Section 5: Maternal and Child Health

Tamil Nadu has achieved significant improvements in maternal and child health indicators over the past two decades. The state's Maternal Mortality Ratio (MMR) and Infant Mortality Rate (IMR) are among the lowest in India. Nearly all institutional deliveries (over 99 percent) in Tamil Nadu take place in hospitals, minimising complications during childbirth. Nutrition rehabilitation centres operate to address severe acute malnutrition in children under five years of age.

## Section 6: Mental Health and NIMHANS Collaboration

The Tamil Nadu government has strengthened its mental health infrastructure through District Mental Health Programmes (DMHP) operating across all 38 districts. Community mental health workers and psychiatrists conduct outreach camps in rural areas. Tamil Nadu collaborates with the National Institute of Mental Health and Neuro Sciences (NIMHANS) and other institutions for capacity building of mental health professionals in the state.
