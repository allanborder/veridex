# Tamil Nadu — Digital Governance and eSevai

## Section 1: Overview of Digital Governance

Tamil Nadu is one of the leading states in India in terms of digital governance and e-government service delivery. The state has developed an extensive network of digital platforms, citizen service centres, and online portals to deliver government services efficiently and transparently. Tamil Nadu's Information Technology sector, centred in Chennai, also contributes significantly to the development of digital governance solutions.

## Section 2: eSevai Centres

eSevai (meaning "e-Service" in Tamil) is Tamil Nadu's flagship citizen service delivery network. eSevai centres are Common Service Centres (CSCs) operated by trained Village Level Entrepreneurs (VLEs) to provide government services to citizens at the doorstep, particularly in rural and semi-urban areas. eSevai centres offer over 300 government services including issuance of birth and death certificates, community and nativity certificates, income certificates, land ownership records (patta), driving licence renewals, property tax payments, and social welfare applications. As of 2025, Tamil Nadu operates more than 4,500 eSevai centres across the state.

## Section 3: Tamil Nadu e-District Portal

The Tamil Nadu e-District Portal (edistrict.tn.gov.in) provides citizens with online access to various district-level administrative services. Citizens can apply for certificates, check application status, and receive digitally authenticated certificates through the portal without visiting government offices in person. The e-District portal is integrated with DigiLocker, allowing citizens to store and access their digital certificates and documents through the national platform.

## Section 4: TNEGA and Government IT Infrastructure

Tamil Nadu e-Governance Agency (TNeGA) is the nodal agency responsible for planning, designing, and implementing e-governance projects in Tamil Nadu. TNeGA maintains the State Wide Area Network (SWAN), the State Data Centre (SDC), and the State Service Delivery Gateway (SSDG), which are critical components of Tamil Nadu's digital infrastructure. TNeGA also oversees the GIS-based systems used for land records, flood management, and urban planning.

## Section 5: Digital Payments and Jan Dhan Integration

Tamil Nadu has promoted digital payments among citizens and government service recipients. Social welfare transfers including pensions, scholarship disbursements, and agricultural subsidy payments are made directly to beneficiaries' bank accounts through the Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) framework. The state government has integrated its welfare delivery systems with the Jan Dhan-Aadhaar-Mobile (JAM) trinity to reduce leakages and ensure targeted delivery of benefits.

## Section 6: Departments Involved in e-Governance

Multiple departments of the Tamil Nadu government are actively involved in the delivery of digital governance services. The Revenue Department manages land records digitisation and certificate issuance. The Agriculture Department operates the Farmer Registry and online insurance portals. The Health Department uses digital platforms for telemedicine and medicine supply chain tracking. The Social Welfare Department administers pension and scholarship disbursements digitally. The Commercial Taxes Department operates an online GST and tax payment portal. The School Education Department manages student enrolment data and mid-day meal beneficiary lists digitally.
