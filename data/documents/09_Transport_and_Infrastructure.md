# Tamil Nadu — Transport and Infrastructure

## Section 1: Road Network

Tamil Nadu has an extensive road network that is one of the densest in India. The state has more than 200,000 kilometres of roads, including National Highways, State Highways, Major District Roads, and Village Roads. The National Highways Development Project has added several expressways and four-lane highways connecting major cities in Tamil Nadu. The Chennai Outer Ring Road and the Chennai Bypass are critical arterial routes that reduce congestion in the metropolitan area. The Tamil Nadu Highways Department (TNHD) is responsible for the construction and maintenance of state highways.

## Section 2: Rail Transport

Tamil Nadu is well-connected by rail under the Southern Railway zone of Indian Railways, headquartered in Chennai. Chennai Central and Chennai Egmore are the two major railway terminals in the state capital. Tiruchirappalli, Madurai, Coimbatore, and Salem are important railway junctions. Tamil Nadu is served by several express and superfast trains connecting it to major cities across India including Mumbai, Delhi, Kolkata, and Bengaluru. Chennai's suburban rail network, operated by Indian Railways, serves millions of daily commuters across the Chennai Metropolitan Area.

## Section 3: Chennai Metro Rail

The Chennai Metro Rail project is a mass rapid transit system serving the Chennai metropolitan area. Phase 1 of the Chennai Metro Rail covers two corridors: Corridor 1 from Washermanpet to Chennai Airport, and Corridor 2 from Chennai Central to St. Thomas Mount. Phase 2 of the Chennai Metro Rail is a major expansion project that will add approximately 118.9 kilometres of elevated and underground metro rail lines, connecting additional suburban areas of Chennai including Sholinganallur, Siruseri, Poonamallee, and Wimco Nagar. The Phase 2 project aims to significantly reduce road traffic congestion and improve public transport options in Chennai.

## Section 4: Chennai International Airport

Chennai International Airport (Meenambakkam) is one of the four major international airports in India. It serves as the primary gateway for air travel to and from Tamil Nadu and handles millions of passengers annually. A new integrated terminal building has been constructed to expand capacity and modernise facilities. A greenfield airport project is under development at Hosur in Krishnagiri district to serve the growing aviation demand in southern Tamil Nadu. Tamil Nadu also has domestic airports at Coimbatore, Madurai, Tiruchirappalli, and Thoothukudi.

## Section 5: Port Infrastructure

Tamil Nadu has several major seaports that are critical to the state's trade and commerce. Chennai Port Trust (Madras Port) is one of the largest artificial harbours in Asia and handles container, bulk cargo, and petroleum shipments. V.O. Chidambaranar Port (Thoothukudi) is the second largest port in Tamil Nadu and handles a significant volume of cargo. Kamarajar Port (Ennore Port), located north of Chennai, is India's first corporatised major port and handles coal, petroleum, and container cargo. Colachel Port in Kanyakumari district is being developed as a major transshipment hub.

## Section 6: Power and Energy Infrastructure

Tamil Nadu has been a frontrunner in renewable energy development in India. The state has one of the largest installed capacities of wind energy in the country, with wind farms concentrated in the Tirunelveli, Thoothukudi, and Coimbatore regions. Tamil Nadu also has significant solar energy installations. The Tamil Nadu Generation and Distribution Corporation (TANGEDCO) manages power generation and distribution across the state. Tamil Nadu has achieved near-universal household electrification, with power supply reaching even remote villages through various rural electrification programmes.
