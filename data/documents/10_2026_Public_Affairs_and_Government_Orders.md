# Tamil Nadu — 2026 Public Affairs and Government Orders

## Section 1: Overview of 2026 Policy Agenda

In 2026, the Tamil Nadu government continued its focus on inclusive development, welfare delivery, and economic growth. The state's policy agenda centred on agricultural support, infrastructure expansion, women's empowerment, health coverage, and digital governance. A number of important government orders, cabinet decisions, and scheme announcements were made during the first half of 2026.

## Section 2: Kuruvai Agricultural Package Announcement

In May 2026, the Tamil Nadu state government announced a special support package for Kuruvai paddy cultivation in the Cauvery Delta districts. The Kuruvai Special Package of Rs. 500 crore was approved by the state cabinet and announced by the Chief Minister. This package was intended to compensate farmers who could not cultivate due to insufficient Cauvery water releases and to provide input subsidies to those who proceeded with cultivation using alternative water sources such as borewells and tanks. The package covered five Cauvery Delta districts: Thanjavur, Tiruvarur, Nagapattinam, Mayiladuthurai, and Cuddalore.

## Section 3: Farmer Registry Deadline Announcement (May 2026)

The Tamil Nadu government issued a government order in May 2026 announcing a deadline for the Farmer Registry enrolment programme. All farmers who wish to avail themselves of direct benefit transfers, agricultural subsidies, and crop insurance under state schemes are required to complete their registration under the Farmer Registry by June 30, 2026. The government order specified that farmers who miss the deadline may face a disruption in the receipt of agricultural welfare benefits until their registration is completed. The Agriculture Department was directed to conduct special registration camps across all taluks to facilitate enrolment.

## Section 4: Tamil Nadu Investment Promotion

In 2026, Tamil Nadu continued to attract significant domestic and foreign direct investment. The Tamil Nadu Global Investors Meet (GIM) has been a platform for attracting large-scale investments into manufacturing, IT, renewable energy, and infrastructure sectors. Several major semiconductor, electronics, and electric vehicle manufacturing projects were announced for Tamil Nadu following agreements with domestic and international companies. The Tamil Nadu Industrial Guidance and Export Promotion Bureau (TIDCO and SIPCOT) manages industrial estates and promotes investment in the state.

## Section 5: Infrastructure and Urban Development Orders

The state government issued several key orders in early 2026 related to infrastructure development. Approval was granted for the Phase 2 expansion of the Chennai Metro Rail project. Orders were issued for the construction of new elevated highways and flyovers in Chennai to ease traffic congestion. The Smart City Mission projects in Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, and Tiruchirappalli received state government co-funding approvals. Urban flooding mitigation works for the Adyar and Cooum rivers in Chennai were fast-tracked following the floods of previous years.

## Section 6: Social Welfare Scheme Expansions

In 2026, the Tamil Nadu government expanded several social welfare schemes. The Pudhumai Penn scheme for girl students was extended to cover students in aided private colleges in addition to government colleges. The Chief Minister's Breakfast Scheme was expanded to cover Classes 1 to 8 in government schools, up from Classes 1 to 5 in the earlier phase. The Kalaignar Magalir Urimai Thittam (Women's Rights Scheme), which provides monthly financial assistance to eligible women heads of households, continued its implementation with additional beneficiaries enrolled through the state's welfare database.
