# VERIDEX Demo Knowledge Base — Attendance Requirements
# Academic Standing Policy Memo

## Section 1: Mandatory Minimum Attendance
In accordance with academic council mandates, every enrolled student must maintain a minimum mandatory attendance threshold of 75% in every registered lecture, tutorial, and practical course to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examinations.

## Section 2: Medical Condonation
Students with aggregate attendance between 65% and 74.9% due to documented hospitalization or severe medical indisposition may petition the Dean of Academic Affairs for condonation, provided official medical certificates and hospital discharge summaries are submitted within 5 business days of resuming campus classes. No condonation is granted for attendance below 65%.
