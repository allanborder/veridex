# Clearly labeled illustrative documents for HackWithAMYPO 2026 evaluation.

## Section 1: Library Operating Hours
The Central Campus Library operating schedule during regular academic terms is strictly established as follows:
- Monday through Friday: 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
- Saturday and Sunday: 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
Circulation and physical borrowing services terminate 30 minutes prior to building closure. The library does NOT close at 8:00 PM on weekdays under any regular circumstance.

During mid-term and final examination weeks, the Quiet Study Hall in Block C remains accessible 24/7 for authorized enrolled students possessing active biometric digital IDs.

## Section 2: Circulation and Borrowing Quotas
Undergraduate students may check out a maximum of 4 physical volumes simultaneously for a loan period of 14 calendar days. Graduate researchers and faculty members are permitted up to 10 volumes for 30 calendar days. Overdue fines accrue at a rate of 5.00 currency units per working day.
