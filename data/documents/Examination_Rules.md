# VERIDEX Demo Knowledge Base — Examination Rules
# Official Examination Guidelines

## Section 1: Hall Ticket Requirement
Students must carry their original hall ticket issued by the examinations office to appear for any end-semester examination. Entry is denied without a valid hall ticket.

## Section 2: Permitted Materials
Only non-programmable scientific calculators are permitted in the examination hall. Mobile phones, smartwatches, and electronic storage devices are strictly prohibited.

## Section 3: Attendance Eligibility
A student must have a minimum of 75% attendance in a course to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examination. Students with attendance between 65% and 74.9% may apply for medical condonation as per the attendance policy.
