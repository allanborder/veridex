# VERIDEX Demo Knowledge Base — Institutional Merit & Need Scholarships

## Section 1: Eligibility and Application Documents
Institutional merit-cum-need scholarships provide full or partial tuition waiver grants to deserving undergraduate scholars.
Mandatory documentation required for application filing:
1. Prior academic semester transcript demonstrating a Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of at least 8.50 on a 10.0 scale.
2. Official Annual Family Income Certificate issued by the designated revenue authority (not exceeding 300,000 annual income).
3. Recommendation letter signed by the Departmental Head of Department (HOD).
4. Photocopy of national identity card and active institutional bank account passbook.

Applications close on October 15 annually at 17:00 IST.
