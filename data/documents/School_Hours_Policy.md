# Official School Hours Policy

## Section 1: School Operating Hours
The school operates Monday through Friday. School hours are from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM on regular weekdays. Students must be present by 8:00 AM for morning assembly.

## Section 2: Saturday Schedule
On Saturdays, the school operates from 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM for co-curricular activities and remedial sessions only.

## Section 3: Holiday and Special Days
The school remains closed on all gazetted national holidays and during scheduled semester breaks as per the academic calendar issued at the start of each academic year.
